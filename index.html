Frequently asked questions

Download, who it is for, stroke-order standards and device support.

Who is Character Assembly for?

Adults and teenagers starting Chinese, adults who want to fix their stroke order or revisit idioms, casual players who enjoy word puzzles, and parents practising with their children. All content is common characters, high-frequency idioms and texts that appear in school material.

Is the tracing based on standard stroke order?

Yes. Stroke order comes from standard stroke-order data, with manual corrections for easily confused shapes such as 横撇, 竖折, 横折钩, 竖弯钩, 卧钩, 点 and 捺. A wrong stroke is flagged immediately instead of being caught at the end.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

Neither. Levels, audio and progress are stored on the device, so you can practise offline. The app asks for no registration and progress is never uploaded.

Does it support iPad and an English interface?

Yes. iPad portrait follows the phone layout, while landscape puts the display and working area side by side. The interface switches between Simplified Chinese and English, and idiom meanings include English explanations.

How much content is there?

60 character levels × 10 characters (600 in total), 50 idiom levels × 4 four-character idioms (200 in total) and 24 classical texts, including 木兰辞, 马说, 将进酒, 桃花源记, 蜀道难, 爱莲说, 陋室铭, 岳阳楼记 and 出师表.

Where can I download it?

Search for “趣学汉字” or “Fun Hanzi” on the App Store, or open the App Store page. It runs on iPhone and iPad.

Designed for mobile learning and casual puzzling

The flow favours quick starts, clear goals and immediate feedback. Finishing a level unlocks the next one, and classical texts can be resumed — good for commutes, after-school practice or playing together with family.
